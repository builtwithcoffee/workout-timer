Quick start & how it works

Workout Timer Guide

A local-first companion for choosing, running, and logging your BuiltSimple workouts on an iPhone or iPad.

Back to timer

Quick start

  1. 1

    Select your track

    On first use, choose the BuiltSimple track you are following. Workout Timer remembers it on this device until you choose Change.

  2. 2

    Choose today’s workout

    Select Lower Body, Dip, Pull, or Push Focus Day. The shared Cardio · Burpees workout appears once under Optional.

  3. 3

    Review your workout

    Check that the exercises, sets, targets, rests, and block order match your current assignment, then choose Start workout.

  4. 4

    Follow the timer and log the result

    Enter the actual reps and weight for manual work, then tap Set done. After finishing, review the results and save the session to History.

  5. 5

    Use or back up the result

    Copy the concise workout overview for an activity description. Export History separately when you want a complete backup.

Included workouts

This is an early community test of Workout Timer as a BuiltSimple companion. The current library includes the Cycle 4, Week 3 workouts from Momentum and Rise. Forge is available as an importable workout pack. Foundation and Built workouts are not yet included.

The supplied workouts were added manually for this test. Workout Timer does not connect to BuiltSimple or update its programming automatically, so always treat your current BuiltSimple assignment as authoritative.

Changing track is safe: It changes the workout library you are browsing. The workout already open, its edits, and your History remain untouched until you select a different workout day.

Import a weekly workout pack

A workout pack contains several workout days in one file. Save the supplied .workout-pack.json file to your device, open the workout chooser, and tap Import workout pack. Select the downloaded file; its track and workout days become part of your library.

Use Workout pack within the track to choose an edition. Importing next week's pack keeps earlier editions available, and importing the same file again keeps your edits. Importing a pack or changing its edition does not replace the workout already open; selecting a workout day does.

Use Edit workout to enter the rep targets from your assignment wherever the template says PR-based reps. These are instructions, not automatically calculated targets. Edits to imported days are saved separately for each pack and stay available when you switch days or reopen offline.

Keep personal copies: Use More workout tools → Workout files → Save to export an edited day. Remove pack removes that edition and its saved edits from this device after confirmation. Your current workout and History remain.

Prepare your current week

  1. 1

    Confirm the assignment

    Check your current track, week, and workout day in BuiltSimple.

  2. 2

    Open the matching track

    If Workout Timer is showing another track, choose Change and select the one you are currently following.

  3. 3

    Load the workout day

    Select the matching Focus Day and compare its exercises, targets, and rests with the current assignment before training.

  4. 4

    Edit only when needed

    Choose Edit workout to adjust blocks, exercises, sets, targets, rests, timers, or added weight when your assigned version differs. Leave added weight blank for bodyweight sets.

  5. 5

    Save changes you want to keep

    Open More workout tools and choose Save to keep a portable .workout.json copy. Use Load there to restore it later.

Included workouts and individual files: Save an edited copy before choosing another day. Reselecting an included workout reloads its supplied version. Workouts imported as a pack keep their edits in the pack library automatically.

While you train

Manual sets

Enter the completed reps and weight, then tap Set done. Rest screens are for recovery.

Structured blocks

Review the next block, then tap Start block. A skipped block is left out of the saved session.

AMRAPs

Let the timer finish or end it early. Record completed full rounds before finishing the workout.

EMOMs

Each minute starts the next activity. Complete its target, then recover until the following minute begins.

Your plans and data

Workout Timer is local-first. It has no account, server, subscription, or analytics. Your selected track, plans, settings, and workout history stay in the browser on this device and are not uploaded by the app.

After the first online launch, the installed app works without gym Wi-Fi or cellular service. A connection is needed to load the site initially, check for app updates, and download new packs. Once a pack is imported, its workouts are available offline.

Workout file

Save and Load handle a reusable plan. The file does not include completed-session history.

History backup

Export history and Import history handle completed sessions. They do not replace the current workout plan.

Copied overview

A concise plain-text summary for sharing elsewhere. It is convenient, but it is not a complete history backup.

Keep a backup: Export History before clearing browser data, replacing a device, or moving your records to another iPhone or iPad.

Install on iPhone or iPad

When you are ready to try Workout Timer at the gym, add it to your Home Screen for quick access and offline use.

  1. 1

    Return to Workout Timer

    Use the button below to open the timer in your browser.

  2. 2

    Open your browser’s Share menu

    Tap the square with an upward arrow in the browser toolbar or menu.

  3. 3

    Add it to your Home Screen

    Choose Add to Home Screen, then tap Add. The timer will open in its own window.

The installation help in Workout Timer also includes these steps and a reminder about local data and backups.
