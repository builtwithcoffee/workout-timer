INSTALLATION

Install on iPhone or iPad

Install it like an app for quick access at the gym. You only need an internet connection for this first visit.

  1. 1
    Open this page in your browser

    Use Chrome, Safari, or your preferred browser on the iPhone or iPad where you want the timer.

  2. 2
    Tap the Share button

    Look for the square with an upward arrow in your browser’s toolbar or menu.

  3. 3
    Choose Add to Home Screen

    You may need to scroll down in the Share menu to find it.

  4. 4
    Tap Add

    Workout Timer will appear on your Home Screen and open in its own window.

Local and offline by design

Your workout plans, settings, and history stay on this device. They are not uploaded to an account or server, and the installed timer works without gym Wi-Fi or cellular service.

When you are connected, the app may check for a newer version. Before clearing browser data or moving devices, use History → Export to make a backup.
